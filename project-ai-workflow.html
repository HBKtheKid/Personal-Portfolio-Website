The Problem

Most design teams treat AI tools as optional extras — something to experiment with, not to build workflows around. When I joined SkinnyCoffee as Creative Lead, the team was producing good work, but slowly. Ideation was the bottleneck. Visual exploration consumed time that should have been spent on execution quality.

The question wasn't whether to use AI — it was how to integrate it with enough structure that it reliably compressed production time, not just occasionally.

The Approach

I designed a 4-phase system: structured brief deconstruction with ChatGPT-4o, visual exploration with Midjourney before opening any Adobe tools, execution in traditional software, and a mandatory brand QA gate before delivery. Each phase had a defined tool, a defined output, and a defined time budget.

Then I trained two junior designers on the system over two weeks — prompt engineering fundamentals, supervised practice on live briefs, and critique sessions focused on when AI output needed to go further.

ChatGPT-4o

Brief Deconstruction

Every brief goes through a structured prompt sequence: extract the core business goal, define the target audience precisely, generate 5 visual direction hypotheses, and identify the top 2 that would most likely perform. Replaces a 90-minute team ideation session with a focused 15-minute process.

The 2 chosen directions from Phase 1 become Midjourney prompts. Generate 8–12 visual concepts before opening Illustrator or Photoshop. Brand QA Gate

Brand QA Gate

Every asset passes through a mandatory brand QA checklist before delivery. AI tools can drift off-brand subtly — wrong font weight, slightly off-palette colour, inconsistent tone. The QA gate catches that before it reaches the client. Non-negotiable when AI is in the workflow.
