The Brief

The Whole Bakery Trading is a Gen Z-focused forex education brand. The challenge was unique: forex is traditionally perceived as complex, risky, and inaccessible — especially to young audiences. This brand needed to feel approachable, vibrant, and credible all at once.

As the sole creative on the account, I was responsible for every visual decision — brand identity, content direction, social templates, and all ongoing design output — over a nearly three-year engagement.

My Approach

01

Audience Research & Brand Positioning

Researched Gen Z visual language — what they engage with on Instagram and TikTok, how they consume educational content, what signals credibility vs. what signals "scam". Used this to define a brand position that felt both trustworthy and culturally relevant.

02

Visual Identity System

Built a complete brand identity: wordmark, icon, full palette (warm tones that feel approachable but sophisticated), and a typography system that balances display weight for impact with body legibility for education content.

03

Content Translation Strategy

Developed a framework for turning complex forex concepts — charts, risk management, entry/exit strategies — into visually digestible infographic-style content that Gen Z audiences could quickly understand and share.

04

Ongoing Social Content

Maintained consistent weekly content output over 34 months — static posts, story templates, educational carousels — while continuously evolving the visual language to stay relevant as platform trends shifted.
