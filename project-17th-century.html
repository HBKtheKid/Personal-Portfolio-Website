The Brief

The 17th Century is a fashion and lifestyle brand with an aesthetic rooted in nostalgia, craft, and detail. The challenge was translating that editorial vision into social-media-native content — formats that work on Instagram Reels and TikTok while still feeling like the brand, not just content for content's sake.

They needed a creative partner who could work across statics, motion, and video simultaneously, maintaining brand cohesion across all channels while keeping up with fast-paced seasonal campaign output.

My Approach

01

Brand Audit & Content Strategy

Mapped the existing content landscape and identified gaps between the brand's visual identity and its social presence. Created a seasonal content calendar framework that aligned campaign shoots with organic social output.

02

Static Campaign Visuals

Designed high-quality still imagery for each seasonal campaign — product flats, lifestyle composites, and editorial-style graphic overlays. Every asset was optimised for feed, story, and paid ad dimensions.

03

Motion & Animated Assets

Produced animated story templates, reel transitions, and short-form video edits using After Effects and Premiere Pro. Motion language was developed as an extension of the still brand identity — consistent timing, easing, and colour.

04

Brand Guidelines Update

Codified the brand's evolving visual identity into an updated set of guidelines covering social templates, colour usage, motion principles, and dos/don'ts for external content creators.
