The Brief

Wild Fashion came to me with no existing brand identity — just a vision for a streetwear label that needed to feel authentic, bold, and built to last. They needed everything: a name treatment, logo system, color palette, typography, apparel graphics, lookbook photography art direction, and packaging.

The goal wasn't just to look good — it was to build brand equity from day one, so that customers could recognize and connect with the label across every touchpoint.

My Approach

01

Brand Discovery

I ran a deep discovery process — competitive audit, audience persona research, mood boarding, and direction exploration. Three distinct visual directions were developed and presented before we locked the final brand language.

02

Logo System & Visual Identity

Built a flexible logo system — primary logo, secondary mark, and icon variant — with full usage rules. Typography pairing was chosen for legibility at both large display sizes and small garment labels.

03

Apparel Graphics & Packaging

Designed graphic tees, hoodie prints, and cap embroideries for four collections. Simultaneously developed packaging — hangtags, poly bags, tissue paper — that extended the brand into physical touchpoints.

04

AI-Assisted Campaign Visuals

Used ChatGPT-4o for campaign concept ideation and Midjourney for visual exploration of campaign directions before committing to production. This reduced the pre-production phase by 60% on each launch.
