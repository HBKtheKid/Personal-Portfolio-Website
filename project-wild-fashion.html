Wild Fashion

Full visual identity built from scratch for a Lagos streetwear brand — logo system, brand language, apparel graphics, lookbooks, and four campaign launches.

Role
Creative Lead & Brand Designer
Duration
Dec 2023 – Mar 2025
Sector
Fashion & Apparel

The Brief

Wild Fashion came in with product ideas and a name — no visual identity, no brand voice, no design system. The brief was to build everything from zero: a logo that could live on clothing tags, social media, and packaging, a brand system that could scale across multiple seasonal drops, and campaign visuals that felt editorial rather than transactional.

My Approach

01

Brand Strategy & Positioning

Before any visual decisions, defined the brand positioning: raw, confident, Lagos-bred streetwear for a gen-Z audience. That directional anchor shaped every visual choice after it.

02

Logo & Identity System

Designed three logo variants — primary wordmark, icon mark, and reversed — all built around a single typographic treatment using Syne. The system works at scale from hangtag to billboard.

03

AI-Assisted Campaign Ideation

Used ChatGPT-4o for campaign naming and caption strategy, and Whisk/Midjourney for visual direction exploration — compressing concept-to-campaign time significantly.

04

Apparel & Packaging Graphics

Designed all graphics for SS25 and AW24 collections — tee prints, hoodie back prints, hangtag layouts, and packaging wrap — maintaining brand cohesion across 4 drops.
