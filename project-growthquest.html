The Context

GrowthQuest is an agency serving clients across e-commerce, SaaS, local business, and high-ticket verticals. The challenge: produce a consistent, high volume of performance-focused creative across very different brand voices and client needs — simultaneously, without quality slipping on any account.

How I Managed It

01

Client-Specific Brand Systems

Built modular Figma templates for each client account so assets could be produced rapidly without starting from scratch — consistent brand expression at volume.

02

Vertical-Specific Creative Strategy

E-commerce ads needed urgency and product focus. SaaS needed feature clarity. High-ticket needed trust and authority. Each vertical had a different hook strategy applied consistently.

03

AI-Accelerated Asset Production

Used Midjourney and ChatGPT-4o to accelerate concept exploration and copy hooks — cutting per-asset production time while maintaining quality across 10+ brands.

04

Onboarding & Landing Page Design

Built full brand identities and landing pages for 5+ local businesses — a SaaS client's onboarding redesign drove a 35% increase in trial-to-paid conversion.
