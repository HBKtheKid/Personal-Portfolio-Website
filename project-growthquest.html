GrowthQuest is a performance-focused creative agency. My role is to manage the full creative output for multiple clients simultaneously — ad creatives, brand assets, social media content, and short-form video — all to deadline, all to a consistent standard.

This is the most demanding operational role I've held, and it's where my systems-level thinking, AI-accelerated production, and project management discipline all come together under real pressure.

My Approach

01

Client Brief Intake System

Built a standardised brief intake and creative production system that lets me absorb new client briefs rapidly and produce on-brand assets without constant back-and-forth. This was key to scaling to 10+ clients.

02

AI-Accelerated Ideation

Midjourney and ChatGPT-4o are embedded in every project — from initial concept generation to copy hooks for ad creatives. I estimate AI tools cut my ideation phase by 50% per client, freeing time for execution quality.

03

Weekly Creative Sprints

Operate in weekly sprints with structured check-ins — design, review, revise, deliver. Every client gets a dedicated batch delivery slot and a shared asset library maintained in real-time.

04

Performance Feedback Loop

For clients with active paid campaigns, I track ad performance and feed the data back into creative decisions. The 35% trial-to-paid improvement for the SaaS client came directly from iterating on hook-based creative after analysing CTR data.
