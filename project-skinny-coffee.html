The Brief

SkinnyCoffee Superboost is a US e-commerce brand in the health and wellness space. When I joined as Senior Creative Lead, they needed one person who could own the entire creative pipeline — not just design individual assets, but set brand direction, lead team output, and connect every creative decision back to conversion data.

The challenge: high volume creative demand across Meta ads, email, Amazon storefronts, and landing pages — with a small team and a need to scale output without sacrificing quality.

My Approach

01

Brand Audit & Direction

I started by auditing the existing creative — identifying inconsistencies in tone, visual language, and messaging hierarchy. I then established brand standards and a modular creative system that the whole team could work from.

02

AI-Accelerated Production

I introduced Midjourney, ChatGPT-4o, and Adobe Firefly into the workflow — not to replace design thinking, but to compress ideation time and generate visual concepts at speed. This directly drove the 150% output increase.

03

Meta Ad Creative Strategy

Working directly with the media buying team, I created iterative ad creative variations — different hooks, visual formats, and CTA approaches — and fed conversion data back into the design process. The result was a systematic, data-backed creative loop.

04

Email & Funnel Design

I owned the full Klaviyo email design suite — welcome flows, post-purchase sequences, promotional campaigns — and the corresponding landing pages and funnel pages in Replo and Shopify.

05

Team Leadership & Mentoring

I trained two junior designers on AI tools and established a review system that maintained brand quality while removing bottlenecks. This alone improved team output speed by 40%.
